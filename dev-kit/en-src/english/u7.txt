1. The adjective and the noun for dimensions <span class="ref">p. 53</span>

عندما نصف منشأة نذكر أبعادها: الطول والعرض والارتفاع والعمق. ولكل بُعد كلمتان، صفة واسم، ولكل منهما مكانه في الجملة؛ فإذا وضعت إحداهما مكان الأخرى صارت الجملة خطأ.
When we describe a structure, we give its dimensions: length, width, height and depth. Each dimension has two words, an adjective and a noun, and each has its place in the sentence. If you put one in the place of the other, the sentence is wrong.

<b>الصفة</b> تأتي بعد الرقم والوحدة، فيكون الترتيب: الرقم ← الوحدة ← الصفة.<br><span class="en" lang="en">The tunnel is 9 km long.</span>
<b>The adjective</b> comes after the number and the unit, so the order is: number → unit → adjective.<br><span class="en" lang="en">The tunnel is 9 km long.</span>

الصفة
Word

<b>الاسم</b> يأتي بعد <span class="en" lang="en">The</span> ويتبعه <span class="en" lang="en">of</span> ثم الشيء، ويكون الرقم في آخر الجملة.<br><span class="en" lang="en">The length of the tunnel is 9 km.</span>
<b>The noun</b> comes after <span class="en" lang="en">The</span> and is followed by <span class="en" lang="en">of</span> and the thing. The number is at the end of the sentence.<br><span class="en" lang="en">The length of the tunnel is 9 km.</span>

الأبعاد في جداول المواصفات تُكتب بأرقام ووحدات مختصرة، لكننا نقرؤها كلمات كاملة.
In specification charts, dimensions are written with numbers and short units, but we read them as full words.

<b>الاختصار لا يُجمع:</b> نكتب <span class="en" lang="en">100 m</span> و <span class="en" lang="en">100 km</span>، ولا نكتب <span class="en" lang="en">100 ms</span>.
<b>The short form has no plural:</b> we write <span class="en" lang="en">100 m</span> and <span class="en" lang="en">100 km</span>, not <span class="en" lang="en">100 ms</span>.

الاختصار لا يُجمع:
The short form has no plural:

<b>عند القراءة نجمع الوحدة</b> إذا كان الرقم أكثر من واحد:<br><span class="en" lang="en">100 m = one hundred metres</span><br><span class="en" lang="en">1 m = one metre</span>
<b>When we read it, the unit is plural</b> if the number is more than one:<br><span class="en" lang="en">100 m = one hundred metres</span><br><span class="en" lang="en">1 m = one metre</span>

عند القراءة نجمع الوحدة
When we read it, the unit is plural

<b>الإملاء:</b> البريطانية <span class="en" lang="en">metre, centimetre, millimetre</span>، والأمريكية <span class="en" lang="en">meter, centimeter, millimeter</span>، وكلاهما صحيح.
<b>Spelling:</b> British <span class="en" lang="en">metre, centimetre, millimetre</span>, American <span class="en" lang="en">meter, centimeter, millimeter</span>. Both are correct.

كثيراً ما نطلب شيئاً أو نسأل عنه دون أن نذكر عدده بالضبط، كأن تقول «أحتاج مسامير» أو «هل عندكم أسمنت؟». هنا نستعمل <span class="en" lang="en">some</span> أو <span class="en" lang="en">any</span>، ومعناهما «بعض» أو «أي». ويأتيان مع الجمع المعدود (<span class="en" lang="en">nails</span>) ومع غير المعدود (<span class="en" lang="en">cement</span>)، ويتحدد أيهما نستعمل حسب نوع الجملة.
We often order or ask about something without saying the exact number, like "I need nails" or "Do you have cement?". Here we use <span class="en" lang="en">some</span> or <span class="en" lang="en">any</span>. They come with plural countable nouns (<span class="en" lang="en">nails</span>) and with uncountable nouns (<span class="en" lang="en">cement</span>), and which one we use depends on the kind of sentence.

أولاً: في الجملة العادية (ليست سؤالاً)
First: in a normal sentence (not a question)

<b>الجملة المثبتة</b> تأخذ <span class="en" lang="en">some</span>:<br><span class="en" lang="en">I need some nails.</span><br><span class="en" lang="en">We have some cement.</span>
<b>A positive sentence</b> takes <span class="en" lang="en">some</span>:<br><span class="en" lang="en">I need some nails.</span><br><span class="en" lang="en">We have some cement.</span>

الجملة المثبتة

<b>A negative sentence</b> takes <span class="en" lang="en">any</span>:<br><span class="en" lang="en">We don't have any nails.</span><br><span class="en" lang="en">There isn't any cement.</span>
