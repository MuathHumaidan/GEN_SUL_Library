<b>الرد:</b> أول من يحيّي يقول <span class="en" lang="en">Nice to meet you.</span> بلا too، ومن يرد عليه يضيف too: <span class="en" lang="en">Nice to meet you, too.</span> أو <span class="en" lang="en">Good to meet you, too.</span>
<b>Answer:</b> the person who speaks first says <span class="en" lang="en">Nice to meet you.</span> with no too, and the person who answers adds too: <span class="en" lang="en">Nice to meet you, too.</span> or <span class="en" lang="en">Good to meet you, too.</span>

الأول بلا too، والرد عليه يضيف too (أو Good to meet you, too).
The first person says it with no too, and the answer adds too (or Good to meet you, too).
